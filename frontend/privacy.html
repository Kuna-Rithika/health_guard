Privacy Policy

How HealthGuard collects and protects your data.

Privacy is our priority

HealthGuard respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains the types of data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Basic account information such as name, email, and user ID.
  • Health input and symptom data needed for analysis.
  • Usage preferences to personalize the app experience.

How we use it

  • To generate health reports and recommendations.
  • To keep your preferences saved across sessions.
  • To improve our service without sharing personal details.

Security

We store data locally and use best practices to keep it safe. Sensitive data is never shared without your consent.
