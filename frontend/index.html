About HealthGuard

Not a symptom checker.
A digital health team.

Most health apps give you a static checklist. HealthGuard thinks, remembers, and evolves with you - across every session.

HealthGuard is a voice-driven multi-agent AI health assistant that transforms how you understand your own body. Describe your symptoms naturally - by typing or speaking - and 8 AI agents spring into action.

Unlike one-size-fits-all symptom checkers, HealthGuard remembers your history. Using Qdrant vector memory, it finds recurring patterns, tracks trends over time, and personalizes every recommendation specifically to you.

Whether you're experiencing mild fatigue that needs a lifestyle nudge or severe chest pain demanding emergency guidance - HealthGuard triages intelligently and responds appropriately.

Built for the real world: preventive before reactive. Catch health issues weeks before they escalate into crises.

🎙

Voice-First Input
